Privacy Policy

Updated September 22, 2026

This policy applies to the CleanPhoto iPhone app. Developer and support contact: tatacheung9274@gmail.com.

Photo access

CleanPhoto uses iOS photo permissions to browse, favorite, share, and delete authorized photos on your device. You can allow selected photos only and change or revoke access in system settings. The app reads photos and necessary information such as creation dates. It does not upload photos or photo metadata to developer servers. Actual deletion requires iOS system confirmation.

Local organizing history

Browsing mode, position, photo identifiers, deletion marks, and the last 30 undo steps are stored on your device to restore your progress. The organizing history directory is excluded from iCloud backup; settings are stored in app preferences. Uninstalling removes local organizing history but does not reverse favorite or deletion changes already made in Photos. Recovery and retention of deleted photos depend on their availability in Recently Deleted in Photos.

iCloud and sharing

Only local previews load by default. Enabling originals or explicitly sharing a photo may cause the system to download it from your iCloud Photos, potentially using cellular data. CleanPhoto has no separate photo upload server. You choose recipients through the system share sheet; recipients process shared content under their own privacy policies.

Free access for mainland China accounts

Mainland China App Store accounts can use all features for free indefinitely, without a trial or purchase. The app reads and caches the storefront country supplied by Apple StoreKit on your device for offline access decisions. It does not infer this from location or device language or send it to the developer. When StoreKit reports a different storefront, that region’s access rules apply. Other storefronts use the trial and purchase rules below.

Trial and purchases

Apple’s App Store handles the free trial and lifetime access. CleanPhoto reads and verifies Apple-provided transaction information on your device to determine trial and purchase access. It does not send transaction records to developer servers, and the developer does not receive payment card details. Apple processes App Store transactions under its policies; the developer may receive sales statistics from Apple. Starting a trial, purchasing, and restoring purchases may require internet access.

Data collection and support

The app requires no app account and includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not send photos, action history, or device identifiers to the developer. If you email support, the developer uses your email address and the information you provide to respond. Please avoid sending unrelated photos or sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address above, except where retention is legally required.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process access logs under its own privacy policy; this does not mean the app uploads photos to the website.

Updates

This policy will be updated when features change, with the effective date shown above. Please email support with any privacy questions.
